FOR MRS LECOMTE'S STUDENTS · ACCESSIBILITY

Use the site your way.

The site includes display and reading options designed to support different visual, motor, attention and reading needs.

♿

Built-in accessibility options

Use the Accessibility button at the bottom of every page, or press Alt + A on a keyboard.

🔎

Text and zoom

Choose larger text sizes. The responsive layout is designed to reflow rather than require horizontal reading at common mobile widths.

📖

Reading & dys support

Enable a simple readable system font, extra line/word/letter spacing, underlined links and an optional reading guide.

◐

Visual contrast

A high-contrast mode strengthens foreground/background contrast, borders and controls. Information is not intended to depend on colour alone.

⌨️

Keyboard access

Navigation, search, flashcards, notebook controls and accessibility options can be used from the keyboard, with a strong visible focus indicator.

🔈

Screen readers

Pages use semantic headings, labelled controls, skip links, status announcements and page landmarks to improve screen-reader navigation.

🖐️

Motor & touch access

Interactive controls use larger touch targets and do not require precise pointer movements. There are no timed vocabulary tasks in the current tools.

🌀

Motion sensitivity

Reduce Motion removes or minimises card flips, smooth scrolling, transitions and decorative movement. The site also respects your device's reduced-motion preference.

💾

Your preferences stay local

Accessibility settings are saved in your browser on this device. They are not sent to Mrs Lecomte or stored in a student database.

Accessibility approach

The site is designed with WCAG 2.2 AA practices in mind, including keyboard use, visible focus, contrast, text resizing, reduced motion and semantic structure. This is an ongoing educational project rather than a formal accessibility certification.

If something on the site creates an accessibility barrier, please tell Mrs Lecomte so the resource can be improved.
