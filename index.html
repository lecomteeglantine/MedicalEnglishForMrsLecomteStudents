ON YOUR PHONE · EVEN OFFLINE

Install it like an app.

Add Medical English for Mrs Lecomte's Students to your phone or tablet home screen. After the first complete visit, the dictionary, flashcards, notebook, games and course pages are available even without an internet connection.

● Online

Android / Chrome: use Install app when offered, or open the browser menu and choose Install app / Add to Home screen.

iPhone / iPad: open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Offline access is prepared automatically after your first visit.

Pronunciation note: the vocabulary itself works offline. The Listen button uses the English speech voice installed in your browser or device, so offline pronunciation availability can depend on the phone.

🔒

PRIVACY FIRST · GDPR

Your study data stays on your device.

No account, analytics or student database is used. Your notebook and flashcard progress are saved only in your browser on this device. They are not sent to Mrs Lecomte, ChatGPT/OpenAI or a server by this site.

Important: never enter patient-identifying or confidential clinical information in your notebook.
