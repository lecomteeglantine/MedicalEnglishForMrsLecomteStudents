Humanitarian Go-Bag Challenge

Pack an emergency mission kit as a team. New information arrives in stages, so you must rethink your choices, protect the essentials and justify every trade-off.

  • Choose 10 items together from a clearly illustrated equipment bank
  • Adapt the bag as flooding, medical needs and access problems develop
  • Prioritise the final kit and prepare one short team briefing
Final group outputA two-minute team briefing. Every student speaks and the group justifies priorities, trade-offs and one contingency plan.
