Which activity are you doing?

The two activities are independent. Open the one assigned by Mrs Lecomte.

👥 Group activity · 3–4 students

Humanitarian Go-Bag Challenge

Work on one shared device. Build an emergency response kit, adapt it as the humanitarian situation changes, and defend your team's priorities in English.

  • Choose exactly 10 items as a team.
  • Adapt the bag after flooding, clinical and access updates.
  • Prioritise resources and justify trade-offs.
  • Finish with a 2-minute team briefing in which everyone speaks.
Start the group challenge →
👤 Individual activity

Humanitarian Field Mission

Work independently through a complete humanitarian response simulation: understand the field situation, make careful decisions and practise the English of humanitarian medicine.

  • Build humanitarian and displacement vocabulary.
  • Work through triage, access and logistics decisions.
  • Practise conditionals, word stress and safe reporting.
  • Complete the final “24 Hours to Respond” assignment.
Start the individual challenge →
Important: the group challenge is designed for 3–4 students on one shared device. The individual mission is designed to be completed alone. Each activity keeps its own progress locally in the browser.
