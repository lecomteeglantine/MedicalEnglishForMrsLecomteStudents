FGSM3 · DAY 1 · ONLINE CONSULTATIONS

Choose your activity

Day 1 has two different challenges. Choose the activity your teacher has assigned: work independently in the Teleconsultation Control Room, or work as a team to build and defend an online consultation clinic.

Which activity are you doing? Check the number of students below before you start.
👤 Individual activity 1 STUDENT · 1 DEVICE

Teleconsultation Control Room

Work through the clinical missions independently. Manage patient calls, make safe decisions and revise the language from Day 1.

  • Complete the missions at your own pace
  • Practise clinical questioning, pronunciation and grammar
  • Finish with a complete teleconsultation challenge
START INDIVIDUAL GAME→
👥 Group activity 3–4 STUDENTS · 1 DEVICE

Build Your Online Consultation Clinic

Work as a team. Build a clinic, make difficult choices, spend your budget, respond to patients and survive a crisis.

  • Make decisions together — there is no perfect clinic
  • Balance access, safety, patient experience and efficiency
  • Prepare to defend your choices orally to the class
START GROUP CHALLENGE→
💡
Do not open both activities.Your teacher will tell you whether you are working individually or in a group today.
