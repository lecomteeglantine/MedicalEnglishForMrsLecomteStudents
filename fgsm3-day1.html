👥 Group activity · 3–4 students

Build Your Online Consultation Clinic

Design a primary-care teleconsultation service together. Make strategic choices, balance competing priorities and be ready to justify your decisions.

  • Design access and clinical pathways.
  • Balance budget, safety, access, experience and efficiency.
  • Respond to patient cases and a service crisis.
  • Prepare your final clinic defence as a group.

👤 Individual activity

Teleconsultation Control Room

Work through a clinical communication shift on your own. Make safe teleconsultation decisions while practising the English you need to manage the encounter.

  • Open the call safely and confirm identity and location.
  • Work through patient scenarios and clinical communication choices.
  • Practise pronunciation, grammar and research communication.
  • Complete the Final Live Shift.
