FOR MRS LECOMTE'S STUDENTS · PRIVACY & GDPR

Privacy by design.

This educational website is designed to work without student accounts or a student database.

🔒
🚫

What this site does not do

This site does not ask you to create an account and does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking tools, contact forms or a server-side student database.

💻

What is stored locally

Your personal vocabulary notebook, your own notes, flashcard progress and accessibility display preferences are stored in your browser's local storage on the device you are using.

📡

What is not transmitted

Content you create in the notebook and your flashcard progress are not transmitted to Mrs Lecomte, ChatGPT/OpenAI or a server by this website.

⚕️

No patient data

Do not enter names, dates of birth, hospital identifiers or any other patient-identifying or confidential clinical information in your notebook.

🧹

You stay in control

You can remove individual notebook entries, clear your notebook and flashcard progress, or clear this site's local browser data at any time.

🌐

Hosting

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard technical connection data as part of providing the hosting service, under GitHub's own privacy practices.

Offline app storage

To make the site available offline, your browser may keep a local cached copy of the site's public files on your device. This cache contains the website resources, not a student account or a server-side copy of your notebook.

Pronunciation audio

The Listen buttons use the speech-synthesis functions provided by your browser or device. This site does not use your microphone and does not record your voice.
