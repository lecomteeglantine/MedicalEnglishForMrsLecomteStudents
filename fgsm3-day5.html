BOARD TRAINING

Know what you are rating

Once all four first impressions are saved, the Review Criteria Check opens. This part is scored: identify which aspect of a medical drama is being assessed.

🩺Medical realism

Clinical actions, procedures, investigations, timing and plausibility.

👥Teamwork realism

Who does what, professional roles, handovers and collaboration.

💬Communication

How clinicians speak with patients, relatives and colleagues.

⚖️Ethics

Consent, confidentiality, boundaries, fairness and responsibility.

🎭Entertainment

Pace, suspense, emotional impact and storytelling value.
