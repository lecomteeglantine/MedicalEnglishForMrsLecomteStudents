CONTROL ROOM · SYSTEM 05

Mission 5 · Certainty Calibration

Clinical and scientific language should match the strength of the evidence. Calibrate each claim so it is clear, precise and no more certain than the data allow.

MISSION 5 PROGRESS0 / 4
STRONG DEDUCTIONmust · can’tUse when the evidence strongly supports or rules out an explanation.
POSSIBILITYmay · might · couldUse when the evidence allows an explanation but does not prove it.
PREDICTIONwill · won’tUse for a prediction stated with confidence.
LIKELIHOODis likely to · is unlikely toUse when you want to calibrate a prediction more cautiously.
