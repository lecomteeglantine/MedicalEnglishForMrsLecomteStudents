Group activity · AI Clinical Control

HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP · GROUP MISSION

The AI Hospital Safety Board

Your hospital is deciding how six AI-supported clinical tools should be used. There is no randomisation: every option has a fixed consequence. If two groups make the same choices on different devices, they receive the same path, same scores and same final briefing.

Teams of 3–46 board decisions≈ 20 min discussion2 min per student recap

Student instructions

  1. Form a team of 3 or 4 students. Choose your group size below.
  2. Assign the fixed roles. Do not randomise roles or choices.
  3. Watch or review the Day 4 AI video together. Focus on what the AI does and where human judgement remains necessary.
  4. For each board case, discuss before clicking. Choose one option as a team.
  5. Read the consequence. You cannot change a decision after moving to the next case unless you reset the whole board.
  6. At the end, use the automatically generated briefing. Every student speaks for approximately 2 minutes.
DETERMINISTIC MODE · NO RANDOM CHOICES

Options are never shuffled and the game never uses a random number. The decision code A/B/C fully determines the consequence, scores and final result. Laptop, tablet and phone therefore behave identically for the same choice path.
