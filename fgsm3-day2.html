STOP 04 · SYDNEY

The Bulk Billing Challenge

Clear Toronto to unlock your Australian assignment.

SYDNEY CHECK 0 / 11
AUSTRALIA MEDICARE PUBLIC + PRIVATE TIER
🇦🇺 AUSTRALIA · UNIVERSAL PUBLIC INSURANCE + PRIVATE TIER Fourth assignment: work out when the patient pays — and when Medicare pays the doctor directly.

Public hospital care is free, GP visits and medicines are subsidised, and private cover plays a substantial role alongside Medicare. Your key challenge is to decode bulk billing, gap fees and the PBS.

MODELUniversal + private tier RUN BYMedicare + states COVERAGEUniversal SPEND≈ 10% GDP
MEDICARE LEVYFunding

A dedicated tax on income that helps fund Medicare.

BULK BILLINGNo patient payment

The doctor bills Medicare directly, so the patient pays nothing for that visit.

GAP FEEOut-of-pocket

The extra amount a patient pays above the Medicare rebate.

PBSMedicines

The Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme subsidises medicines.

Final -s Sound Check

Clear Sydney to unlock your communication check before the flight to Wellington.

SOUND CHECK 0 / 12
TRANSIT GATE T2 FINAL -S /ɪz/ · /s/ · /z/
🔊 COMMUNICATION CHECK · HEALTHCARE ENGLISH Listen to the ending, then classify the final -s sound.

The spelling stays visible for accessibility. Focus on the final sound before -s, not on the letter itself. You can hear each word and a short healthcare sentence before answering.

/ɪz/Extra syllable

After s, z, sh, ch or ge sounds: doses, analyses, cases.

/s/Voiceless ending

After p, t, k or f sounds: results, patients, groups.

/z/Voiced ending

After other voiced sounds: findings, values, methods.

🇳🇿 NEW ZEALAND · HEALTH NZ / TE WHATU ORA First decode the system. Then analyse a real leadership crisis reported by RNZ.

New Zealand has universal public cover, free public hospital care, subsidised GP visits with common co-payments, and ACC no-fault injury cover. Your second task is to separate what the RNZ clip reports from political claims and information it does not give.

▶ RNZ · THE DETAIL

New Zealand's health leadership crisis

Watch the 1 min 23 clip before the crisis checkpoints. The game never depends on sound alone: a text alternative summarises the context, but Not Given still means the clip does not state the information.

Read the accessible briefing

The clip reports a succession of senior departures from New Zealand's health leadership. It places these departures against a background of staff shortages, long waits in emergency departments, for GPs and for scans, and hospital “near misses”. It also reports an opposition claim that senior figures are being pushed out and blamed for government failures. The clip does not give an exact number of resignations.

DUBLIN CHECK 0 / 14
GLOBAL HEALTH PASSPORT 🇮🇪 DUBLIN WORKFORCE ASSIGNMENT · STOP 06
IRELAND · HSE · SLÁINTECARE Decode a mixed two-tier system, then investigate Ireland's plan to expand the healthcare workforce.

First understand access, the medical card, private insurance and Sláintecare. Then move to the workforce dashboard: 612 extra annual healthcare-training places, designed to strengthen the domestic pipeline and address staffing shortages.

MODELMixed · two-tier RUN BYHSE ACCESSUniversal · some charges REFORMSláintecare
ACCESS DECODER

What does “two-tier” mean?

Public and private access exist side by side. Many people pay to see a GP; a means-tested medical card gives free GP and hospital care to lower-income patients.

REFORM TRACK

Sláintecare

The reform plan aims to move Ireland towards universal, single-tier care and reduce inequalities between public and private access.
