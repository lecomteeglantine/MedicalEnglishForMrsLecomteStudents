👥 Group activity · 3–4 students

The Patient Test

Build a fictional healthcare system for Medoria, test it on seven patient profiles, survive a national crisis, reform it, then defend your decisions.

  • 1Use one shared device. Take different commission roles and discuss every major decision before anyone clicks.
  • 2Test your system on seven fictional patients, identify who is protected or left at risk, then respond to a national crisis.
  • 3Choose exactly two reforms and compare the patient outcomes before and after.
  • 4Prepare the final Commission Hearing together.
Final group output

Every student speaks for about 2 minutes. Your 3–4 person presentation should therefore last roughly 6–8 minutes. Present your system design, patient impact, crisis/reforms, and final trade-offs.

👤 Individual activity

Healthcare Systems World Tour

Travel through six healthcare systems, complete language control rooms and finish with a policy forum and mystery destination.

  • 1Work alone on your own device and complete all six country stops in the route order shown.
  • 2Answer 68 checkpoints in total, including healthcare-system facts, comparisons, final -s pronunciation and passive voice.
  • 3Points are awarded on the first attempt only. If an answer is wrong, correct it to continue, but the lost point is not restored.
  • 4Complete all earlier missions to enter the Mystery Destination. Then solve its final clues to reveal your destination, final score and 30-second exit briefing.
Individual output

Complete the full route on your own. You are finished when the Mystery Destination is cleared and your final score appears. Then give a 30-second spoken comparison of two systems. Progress is saved locally on this device, so stay on the same device if possible.
